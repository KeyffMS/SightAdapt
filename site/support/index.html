Bug-report checklist

  • SightAdapt version;
  • Windows version;
  • monitor arrangement and scaling;
  • target application and framework when known;
  • target privilege level;
  • exact reproduction steps;
  • whether emergency disable remained available;
  • sanitized logs or paths.
Privacy warning. Do not attach screenshots containing private information or publish unredacted paths, usernames, organization names or confidential application content.
