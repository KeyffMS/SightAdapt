# SightAdapt internal trademark-risk decision

**Original review date:** 2026-07-27  
**Decision updated:** 2026-07-30  
**Reviewed project version:** 0.5.0.50-alpha  
**Issue:** #88  
**Decision owner:** KeyffMS / aiteracja.pl

## Decision

The project will continue using `SightAdapt` and the current eye-based mark under an internal, documented risk acceptance. No external trademark audit, certified register report or attorney opinion is planned.

The project does **not** claim that the name or logo is legally cleared, available for registration, conflict-free, protected or non-infringing.

**Internal risk rating: medium-high for brand distinctiveness and possible association with `SightAid`; accepted for the current project.**

## Scope considered

- active project development and marketing in Poland;
- direct online distribution and marketing in the European Union;
- global availability through the public repository and release artifacts;
- possible future store, sponsorship, paid-support or commercial distribution;
- downloadable Windows accessibility/personalization software.

## Public-source searches performed

The internal review searched:

- `SightAdapt`, `Sight Adapt`, `Sight-Adapt` and wildcard/phonetic variants;
- nearby names including `SightAid` and `Sight-Aid`;
- public web indexes, app stores, source repositories, software directories and company/product references;
- public entry points for EUIPO/TMview, WIPO and the Polish Patent Office.

The review did not produce a certified register export. Public interfaces and ordinary search results are incomplete and can change.

## Findings

### Exact name

No independently indexed third-party software or company using the exact combined spelling `SightAdapt` was identified in the reviewed sources. This does not prove legal availability.

### Similar names

Several products and projects use `SightAid` or `Sight-Aid` for visual-assistance and low-vision software or research, including app-store applications and research systems.

Risk factors:

- identical `Sight` prefix;
- second element beginning with `A`;
- related assistance/adaptation concepts;
- overlapping accessibility audiences;
- overlapping software, app-store and search channels.

The names are distinguishable, but association or confusion remains plausible.

### Distinctiveness

`Sight` refers directly to vision and `Adapt` describes adaptation. The combined name is suggestive and may provide limited exclusivity. The field is crowded with `Sight`, `Vision`, `Eye`, `Aid` and `Assist` names.

### Logo

The current icon is generated by project code and does not appear to be a direct copy. Eye symbols on rounded-square backgrounds are common in accessibility, monitoring and image-processing software.

Internal assessment:

- copyright-copying risk: low based on the reviewed generated geometry;
- trademark/confusion risk: low-to-medium for the icon alone;
- distinctiveness: weak-to-medium.

## Maintainer treatment

The maintainer accepts the recorded brand risk and will:

- use the exact product identity `SightAdapt` consistently;
- use `™`, never `®`, unless an actual registration is later obtained;
- avoid stating that the mark is cleared, registered, unique or conflict-free;
- avoid third-party logos, trade dress and endorsement implications;
- monitor exact and materially similar uses in the same software/accessibility field;
- record concrete confusion, objections, takedowns or conflicting-right claims;
- reconsider the name when evidence changes, not because an external audit is pending.

## Rename triggers

Open a rename decision when:

- a third party presents a credible prior-right claim or platform complaint;
- users materially confuse SightAdapt with another product;
- a store or directory rejects the identity;
- a commercial partner requires a different name;
- the project decides the limited distinctiveness creates unacceptable marketing cost;
- monitoring finds a materially closer exact or similar product in the same channels.

## Fallback naming brief

If a rename becomes necessary:

- prefer coined or arbitrary terms;
- avoid `Sight`, `Vision`, `Eye`, `See`, `Aid`, `Assist` and direct medical terms;
- remain pronounceable in Polish and English;
- check web, repositories, stores, companies and domains before adoption;
- choose a distinctive logo generated or commissioned for the project;
- migrate assembly metadata, executable/package identity and settings paths through a versioned transition.

Previously noted working ideas such as `PanePrism` and `ChromaRelay` are not approved or reserved names.

## Safe public statement

> SightAdapt is an unregistered project mark. An internal public-source review found no exact indexed use of the combined name and identified several similar `SightAid` accessibility projects. The maintainer has accepted the recorded brand risk and does not claim legal clearance or exclusivity.

## Conclusion

The internal decision is to continue with `SightAdapt`, accept the medium-high brand uncertainty, maintain monitoring and avoid unsupported clearance claims. No external trademark audit remains in the project plan.
